# Start here — 45-day solar SaaS challenge

## Completed today

- Built a no-cost, interactive **LeadRescue** prototype.
- Demonstrated enquiry handling, follow-up tracking, lead status, import/export and summary reporting with fictional data.
- Created a sales discovery script and a suggested pilot structure.
- The original prototype was browser-tested. After this design refinement, source-level checks pass; refreshed visual browser testing is still required (see `TEST_NOTES.md`).

## Next 48 hours (business validation)

1. Share the concept deck and **this demo** with your friend who works at a solar installation company.
2. Ask for 15 minutes to watch their actual enquiry process. Request a walkthrough with all customer details hidden.
3. Write down which parts are done through Excel, CRM, WhatsApp or calls.
4. Ask whether the owner would try a limited paid pilot, and who must approve purchasing.
5. Repeat these conversations with two other solar companies before building subscriptions.

## Prioritization rule

A feature request is not proof of demand. Ask: "If we make just this part reliable, would you pay to use it next month?" Record the answer and purchasing process.

## Next engineering milestone

After customer validation, build a secure multi-tenant API, real login, an audited organization database, private storage and tested backups before entering a real customer record. Billing and outbound communications follow once customer terms and compliance are ready.

## What not to do yet

- Do not put customer details in this demo.
- Do not tell anyone automated follow-ups or payments are available.
- Do not spend money on AI APIs, advertising or custom branding before pilot willingness is established.
- Do not promise a revenue or conversion increase based on the sample dashboard.
