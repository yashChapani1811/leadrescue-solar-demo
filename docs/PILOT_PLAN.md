# First paid solar-company pilot — proposal draft

**Offer name:** LeadRescue Follow-up Workflow Pilot

**Objective:** Help a solar installer's team see which enquiries need action, assign ownership, and record what happened. This pilot does NOT promise more leads, sales or installations.

**Duration:** 7–14 days, only after a secure production pilot environment is available.

**Proposed pilot fee to test:** ₹3,000–₹5,000 per company, payable in advance. Treat this as a hypothesis; ask buyers what they would pay.

**Pilot includes:**

- Up to 3 authorized team members
- A limited set of permission-based enquiries
- Lead status and follow-up workflow
- Daily team follow-up queue
- A basic end-of-pilot report
- One feedback call

**Pilot does not include:** Automated bulk WhatsApp outreach, public lead scraping, guaranteed appointments, full solar design, government subsidy approval, or unlimited custom development.

**Before the pilot:** Confirm company authority and permitted data handling; arrange a secure hosted version with authentication, isolated company records, HTTPS, backups, privacy notice, and deletion/export procedures. Don't use the browser demo with real customer data.

**Success measures:** Daily active team use; missed follow-up rate; time spent finding information; number of previously untracked enquiries brought into a managed queue; willingness to continue at an agreed subscription price. Compare to the team's own prior process where possible.

**Price exploration:** Test ₹2,999–₹4,999/month for the first module, then evaluate a larger bundle only if SurveyProof and QuoteFollow solve distinct paid problems. A ₹11,999 bundle is a target to test, not a validated price.

**Go / no-go at the end:** Proceed only if at least two customers agree that the workflow solves a significant issue AND are willing to pay for continued access.
