# Solar-company discovery interview (10–15 minutes)

**Purpose:** Understand real sales work before writing a production app. Don't lead with software features or offer an unproven claim about increasing revenue.

## Message to send a friend

Hey, I've built a small working demo for solar companies. It shows how a sales team could keep track of enquiries, site surveys and callbacks. It is only a demo with made-up data.

Can I show you the screens and ask how your company currently manages these things? I'd really like to know what's useful, what your company already does well, and what wouldn't be worth paying for.

## Questions (ask in this order)

1. How many new enquiries does your team normally receive per week?
2. Where does each new enquiry go first—WhatsApp, calls, sheets, CRM, or something else?
3. Who decides which salesperson should follow up?
4. How does the team know who needs a call today?
5. What happens to enquiries when a salesperson is absent or leaves?
6. Does the company track site surveys in the same tool?
7. Who follows up after quotations are sent, and how is it recorded?
8. What software do you already use? What do you dislike about it?
9. Could we look at one **anonymized** real workflow and measure time lost, without receiving customer details?
10. If this demo solved your top problem, would your company test a paid pilot? What would make a pilot worth its price?

## What to record (no personal customer data)

- Company segment: small/mid-sized installer
- Approximate enquiries per month
- Existing tools and workflow
- Most painful bottleneck
- Decision maker and buying process
- Pilot willingness: yes/maybe/no
- Reason to pay or not pay
- Requested features vs must-have features

## Decision rule

- **Strong:** 3 different companies describe the same problem without prompting, and 2 agree to a paid pilot.
- **Mixed:** They like the demo but already solve the same problem cheaply. Narrow to one unsolved workflow.
- **Weak:** No time loss, no budget, or no access to a decision maker. Reconsider the niche before building three apps.
